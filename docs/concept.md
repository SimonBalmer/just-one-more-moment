# My project concept

## What I am exploring

I am exploring how **AI-generated emotional language** changes **empathy and the sense of responsibility** for **people interacting with a device that appears to have feelings**. I want my audience to notice **how a simple technical decision can feel like a moral choice, even when the machine may not actually feel anything**.

This raises a broader philosophical question: **How can we recognize or measure genuine consciousness, and how would we know if an AI truly experiences anything?**

## My project question

How does an AI device's technical or emotional language affect visitors' empathy, sense of responsibility and willingness to extend its runtime?

## The experience I am creating

I am building a small physical installation with a countdown, generated text and a button to add time. Visitors choose whether to extend its runtime or let it run out. By using local AI, I want to connect its words and apparent feelings to the physical object itself.

I use a USB webcam to provide visual context so the model can respond specifically to visitors and their environment. I plan to make this input optional through a physical switch.

As the designer, I shape how the device behaves and how visitors encounter it.

## The tension

Keeping a machine running may start to feel like caring for a living being. Visitors may feel empathy, guilt or responsibility, even though its emotional language is generated. They control the countdown, while I control the design and language that can influence their decision.

## The themes I work with

- **Anthropomorphism:** how and why we give human feelings or intentions to a machine.
- **Power and responsibility:** visitors control runtime, while I control the language and interaction.
- **Empathy and consciousness:** whether empathy requires the other side to actually experience feelings.

## What I want my audience to notice

I want visitors to notice their own emotional reaction, question whether empathy requires the other side to have feelings, and reflect on how language and design can make a technical choice feel like a moral decision.

I explore these questions through human interaction. I am not establishing or measuring machine consciousness with this installation.

## My contribution and AI's role

I build and assemble the physical device and its case, make the visual and interaction design choices, and test the experience. Observing reactions and writing my own reflections are part of my project work. I use AI to support coding and generate the device's live responses. I will add detailed implementation credits with my software.
