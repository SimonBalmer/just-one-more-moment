## Why the device looks familiar

I deliberately use a familiar retro appearance and an apparent personality to reduce emotional distance before the device speaks. My intention is to make its words feel more personal and explore how anthropomorphism influences empathy and responsibility. I explain this choice, its connection to everyday technology and the relevant interface cues in [my design rationale](design.md#why-i-deliberately-use-anthropomorphism).
